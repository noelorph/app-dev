Spider-Man: No Way Home (2021)

Spider-Man: No Way Home is a superhero movie released in 2021, starring Tom Holland as Peter Parker (Spider-Man). The story follows Peter after his identity as Spider-Man is revealed to the world. He asks Doctor Strange for help to make everyone forget his secret identity, but the spell goes wrong and brings villains from other universes into his world.

The movie is about friendship, responsibility, sacrifice, and making difficult decisions. It shows that being a hero means helping others, even when it comes with personal sacrifices.
